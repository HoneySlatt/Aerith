# Aerith's logo

`aerith.svg` is Aerith's logo: a five-petal flower made of a single folded ribbon, in a gradient from
violet to cyan. It is a raster image of 1254 × 1254 pixels inside an SVG file, not a vector drawing.

It is not under the code's licence, MPL-2.0 ([ADR-0010](../docs/adr/0010-the-logo.md)). You may show
it, unchanged, to refer to Aerith: in an article, a talk, or a page about the program. You may not use
it, or something close to it, as the logo of another project, a fork of Aerith included, nor so as to
suggest that a project is Aerith or endorsed by it.
